Nigerians have been arguing on social media over how much money is needed to enjoy **Detty December**, with proposed budgets ranging from **₦50,000 to ₦15 million**.

The conversation started after financial services company **Cowrywise** asked its followers on X for a reasonable festive season budget. Responses varied widely, reflecting different expectations and spending plans for the December festivities.

One user, **Ernest Onah**, said ₦50,000 would be enough for him, prompting Cowrywise to ask whether he planned to stay indoors throughout the festivities. Another user, **Farouk Umar**, proposed ₦1 million, while **Philip Stanislaus** suggested ₦500,000 and was asked to explain how he arrived at the figure. At the higher end, a respondent identified as **Great~MUHD** suggested ₦15 million as a reasonable amount for Detty December.

Some users appeared less interested in spending on the festivities. One respondent, **VictorBen**, joked that he would rather spend ₦3 million buying a MacBook and sleep throughout December.

Detty December has become a popular expression for Nigeria’s end-of-year entertainment season, characterised by concerts, nightlife, parties, travel, social gatherings and other festive activities. The period, particularly in Lagos, attracts Nigerians in the diaspora and international visitors, while entertainment venues, hospitality businesses and event organisers often experience increased activity.

The latest debate highlights the different financial expectations surrounding the season, as Nigerians weigh the cost of entertainment and social activities against their personal budgets.
