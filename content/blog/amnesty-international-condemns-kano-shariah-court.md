Amnesty International has condemned the sentencing of a 19-year-old TikTok user, **Zainab Abubakar**, by a Sharia court in Kano State to **five months’ imprisonment and 10 strokes of the cane** for allegedly insulting the state’s Hisbah Board and its Commander, Aminu Ibrahim Daurawa. The court, sitting at the Sharada premises of the Kano State Hisbah Board headquarters, delivered the judgement on Wednesday, November 18, 2026.

In a statement, Amnesty International described the sentence as outrageous and questioned the justification for subjecting a young woman to imprisonment and corporal punishment for expressing her opinion.

> “It is flabbergasting that someone can be made to appear before a court and stand trial, let alone serve a sentence and 10 strokes of cane for simply having expressed a strong opinion,” the organisation said.

Amnesty described Zainab’s trial and conviction as the latest development in what it called a sustained and systematic campaign of repression against young people by Kano authorities. It further expressed concern over what it described as the increasing excesses of the Hisbah Board, particularly against young people from less privileged backgrounds.

> “Five months in jail for ‘insulting the head of Hisbah’ is an outrageous sentence. It is also an indication of increasingly lawless excesses of Hisbah targeting youth from less privileged backgrounds,” the statement added.

The human rights organisation maintained that freedom of expression remains protected under international human rights law, including the right to express opinions that may be considered shocking or offensive.

> “The right to freely express one’s views, even if shocking or offensive, is protected by international human rights law. No one should face punishment for freely speaking their minds,” it stated.

Amnesty International called on the Kano State authorities to overturn Zainab’s conviction and secure her immediate and unconditional release.
